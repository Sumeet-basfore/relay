Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We appreciate the efforts of security researchers in identifying vulnerabilities and practicing coordinated disclosure.

Private Disclosure: Do NOT report suspected vulnerabilities via public GitHub issues, PRs, or public channels. Email our security team directly at security@relay.dev.

1. Scope & Target Invariants

In Scope:

  • Circumvention of Cedar default-deny policies or sandbox boundaries.
  • Exfiltration of target credentials from local OS keyring to prompt context or external hosts.
  • Forging or tampering with Action Receipts or SQLite ledger entries without detection.
  • Cross-action reuse or bypass of interactive human /dev/tty approvals.
  • Local console (relay ui) CSRF, DNS rebinding, or authentication bypass.

Out of Scope:

  • Attacks requiring full root/administrator privileges on the target host OS.
  • Physical hardware memory acquisition or cold-boot attacks.
  • Denial of service via intentionally misconfigured user policies.

2. Response & Remediation Targets

  • Acknowledgment: Within 48 business hours of receipt.
  • Triage & CVSS Scoring: Within 7 business days.
  • Coordinated Release: We collaborate with finders to issue a CVE, publish security advisories, and credit researchers publicly.

3. PGP Encryption

For sensitive disclosures, our public PGP key is available upon request via security@relay.dev.
