Relay Commercial Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026 | Version: 1.0.0-DRAFT

The Fundamental Privacy Guarantee:
Local Relay Execution Data (prompts, tool arguments, credentials, receipts, ledger) stays 100% on your device. Relay does NOT collect, observe, transmit, or monetize local execution data.

1. Notice at Collection (California CCPA & General)

  • Categories Collected: Identifiers (name, email, IP address); Commercial Information (purchased subscription tier, seat count); Network Activity (edge server request logs).
  • Purposes: Fulfilling commercial contracts, issuing offline license keys, processing payments via Stripe, delivering support, and mitigating malicious traffic.
  • Sale or Sharing: We do NOT sell personal information and do NOT share data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

When running the Relay open-core binary or local web console (relay ui):

  • No prompt text, model completions, or tool arguments are transmitted.
  • No credentials or API secrets stored in local vaults are sent to Relay.
  • No telemetry, tracking beacons, or analytics pings are embedded in the software.

3. Commercial Data We Collect

Category Data Elements Purpose & Legal Basis
Commercial Accounts Name, work email, organization, license tier Contractual fulfillment; issuing signed license certificates
Billing Metadata Stripe customer ID, country, tax ID (GSTIN/VAT), last 4 digits Contractual necessity; tax compliance (Stripe processes raw cards)
Support Correspondence Email address, ticket history, customer inquiries Fulfilling commercial support agreements and SLAs
Security Vulnerabilities Reporter email, technical vulnerability descriptions Legitimate interest; coordinated vulnerability remediation

4. Statutory Rights & Grievance Redressal

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have statutory rights under applicable privacy laws:

  • India (DPDP Act, 2023 & Rules, 2025): Right to access, correction, erasure, and statutory grievance redressal. You may contact our designated Grievance Officer at grievance@relay.dev.
  • European Union (GDPR): Right of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction under Articles 15–22. Contact privacy@relay.dev.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA): Right to know, delete, correct, and non-discrimination.

5. Contact Information

To exercise statutory privacy rights or submit inquiries: email privacy@relay.dev.
